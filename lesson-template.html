What this lesson practices

Students listen to individual sounds, blend them together orally, and begin connecting that oral practice to a familiar lesson routine.

Materials

  • Video lesson or recorded modeling
  • Printable practice page or word list
  • Optional picture cards or manipulatives

Quick family note

Pause as needed, repeat the oral blending prompts, and keep the session short if the learner needs extra support.

Parent or caregiver instructions

  1. Watch the lesson together the first time.
  2. Pause after examples if the learner needs wait time.
  3. Repeat the lesson later in the week for extra review.

Extra practice activities

  • Say three sounds and have the learner blend them aloud.
  • Use counters or blocks to represent each sound.
  • Practice the same pattern again with two or three new words.
